01Self

A working identity

One person, several ways of paying attention.

I study environmental engineering, make visual things, take photographs, and follow questions that do not stay in one discipline for long.

Freyja is the place where those interests can sit beside each other without being tidied into a job title. It is part notebook, part studio, part evidence of a life still taking shape.
