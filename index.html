About Haga

A patient eye, a curious mind, and a preference for the honest answer.

Purpose

“I want the things I make to be useful, felt, and awake to the world around them. I would rather understand the hard thing than repeat the easy answer.”

Approach

I notice patterns, small shifts, and contradictions, then stay with them long enough for a clearer question to appear.

Current rhythm

Designing with environmental sensitivity, taking photographs, and learning the language of code, one patient experiment at a time.

What guides the work

Look long enough to see what is actually there. Make something that answers to it. Leave room for wonder.

A working compass, not a finished map.

Featured field note

No. 01 · Ongoing

Learning to look before trying to solve.

Engineering trains me to ask how a system works. Photography reminds me to notice who and what it holds. This is a practice of keeping both instincts in the same frame.

Starting question
How might I pay closer attention to a place before deciding what it needs?
Role
Photographer, observer, editor
Materials
Landscape, field life, roadside light

Not a finished answer or a portfolio proof, just a growing record of attention, made close to the places and people around my studies.
